### Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)
```text
MotoLog is an Android ride tracker and logbook built specifically for motorcycle riders. Track your ride distance, live speed, moving time, and routes with a glove-friendly, glanceable design.

Designed for Handlebar and Pocket Use
- High-Contrast Live Screen: Large, bold speed digits and glanceable stats designed for quick checks while mounted on your handlebar.
- Glove-Friendly Controls: Generous 56 dp touch targets and a 2-second Hold-to-Stop gesture with visual feedback to prevent accidental stops from bumps or gloves.
- Lock-Screen Controls: Ongoing notification keeps your live stats accessible and provides Pause/Resume and Stop controls directly from your lock screen.
- Screen-Off Tracking: Reliable foreground tracking keeps recording distance and routes while your phone stays tucked in your jacket pocket with the screen off.

Ride Calculation & Filtering
- Intelligent Noise Filtering: Filters stationary GPS jitter so time at traffic lights does not artificially inflate your distance.
- Dual Average Speeds: View both Moving Average Speed (distance / moving time) and Overall Average Speed (distance / elapsed time) with one tap.
- Auto-Pause: Automatically pauses moving time when stopped below 3 km/h for 8 seconds, and resumes when your speed exceeds 5 km/h.
- Signal Gap Handling: GPS tunnel blackouts and signal losses are clearly identified without inventing false straight-line distances.
- Crash & Process Recovery: If your phone runs out of battery or kills the app mid-ride, MotoLog detects the unfinished ride on next launch and lets you recover your stats.

Interactive Route Maps & Elevation
- Speed-Colored Routes: Review your completed ride polyline colored by speed gradient.
- Speed & Elevation Graphs: Inspect your speed-over-time and elevation profiles with interactive touch crosshairs.
- Barometer Support: Uses your phone's built-in barometric pressure sensor for relative elevation gain and loss, with automatic GPS altitude fallback.

Garage & Maintenance Logbook
- Multiple Motorcycles: Track odometers, rides, and logs separately for each bike in your garage.
- Service Reminders: Set maintenance tasks by distance (km or miles), calendar days, or whichever comes first (engine oil, chain lube, brake fluid, tyres).
- Full-Tank Fuel Logging: Record fuel fills and calculate actual fuel consumption (km/L, L/100km, or MPG).

Your Data Stays on Your Device
- 100% On-Device Storage: Your GPS tracks, speeds, and garage records are stored locally in your device's internal database.
- No Accounts, No Cloud Servers: MotoLog does not require registration, login, or subscriptions.
- No Advertisements: Clean, distraction-free experience with zero ads or tracking SDKs.
- Full Data Export: Export your rides to standard GPX 1.1 or CSV files at any time via Android's Storage Access Framework, or create an all-in-one ZIP backup.

Important Safety Notice:
Speed readings displayed by MotoLog are derived from GPS signals and do not replace your motorcycle's certified speedometer. Always keep your attention on the road and operate your motorcycle safely. Never interact with your device or adjust settings while riding.
```
*(Character count: ~2,850 characters — safely within the 4,000 character limit).*
